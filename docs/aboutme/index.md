About Me
Hello, my name is Riley Gardner. I chose mechanical engineering because I’ve always liked machines and figuring out how things work. I enjoy taking things apart, rebuilding them, and designing things, so mechanical engineering felt like a good fit for me. I especially enjoy working in CAD, and that is the area I could see myself working in professionally. I have experience using AutoCAD, Creo Parametric, and Revit.
This class is my first real hands-on experience with machining tools, so that part is still unfamiliar to me. I’m excited to learn more about machining, get more comfortable using the tools, and build new practical skills. Professionally, I would like to become the kind of engineer who can design a part in CAD and then see that part actually be made, whether through machining, 3D printing, or another process. Being able to follow a design from the first idea to a finished part and then see it used in the system it was made for is something I find really interesting. That full process is a big part of why I want to become an engineer.
Right now, I am still figuring out exactly what area of mechanical engineering I want to work in, but I know I want it to involve design and manufacturing. I also want to gain more hands-on experience so I can better understand how the parts I design are actually made. Overall, I hope to keep building both my design and hands-on skills so I can eventually turn ideas into real, working parts and systems.
